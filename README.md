## About the course

You play the founder-CEO of **Kopi Kaki Co.**, a fictional Singapore kopi startup opening its Tiong Bahru flagship on 1 December 2026, and you use AI agents to help start it. People keep the goals, the judgement and every approval.

Day 1 begins with a brief history of AI, what agentic AI is and how an agent works under the hood. You then compare the always-on "Big Five" assistants by the model inside and what to use each for and set up **Meta Muse** or **OpenAI Dots** as your CEO assistant. In the afternoon you hire your first AI employee, a **Hermes Agent**, entirely in the Hermes Desktop app. You learn what an AI agent is made of, install Hermes, hand it the company rulebook (AGENTS.md, attached with **+ → Folder…**), and give it a model (your **ChatGPT Plus** plan or Nous Portal's free **Solar Mini 4**) and a Telegram bot (one QR code, or manually with BotFather). Then you learn tools versus skills and use them for Gmail (with a daily 8:00 email summary), web design and PPTX, plus Firecrawl web search. Working alone, it then builds the Kopi Kaki website with UX skills from skills.sh and a professional pitch deck with a PPT tool and skill.

Day 2 moves from one agent to a team. You hire five named bots (Rafi, Lin, Farah, Ivy and Pixel) and put them in one **group chat** to write the LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram launch posts. Then one prompt builds a **video production team on a Kanban board** (research, script, voice-over, HyperFrames render, review, then your approval). Finally, **scheduled jobs (crons)** set both teams to work every week, still behind your approval. The day closes with grounding/RAG, evaluation metrics and the five principles of AI security. An optional bonus topic runs Kopi Kaki as a **one-person company in Paperclip**.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Analyse AI (LLM-based) applications across a range of industries to identify their capabilities and limitations.
- **LO2:** Establish the relationship between AI algorithm/agent design and chatbot/agent efficiency.
- **LO3:** Evaluate and improve the effectiveness of AI (RAG and agent) applications in product development.

## Topics covered

1. **Human–AI Collaboration & Always-On AI Agents.** AI history and agentic AI; under the hood (the brain-and-body analogy, the agentic loop); five collaboration scenarios; use cases; the Big Five always-on agents compared by model and use case; Activity 1.
2. **Hermes Agent — Your First AI Employee.** What an AI agent is; what Hermes is; install Hermes Desktop and attach the company folder; provider and model (Nous free Solar Mini 4 or ChatGPT Plus); Telegram by QR code or BotFather; voice; tools vs skills, with Gmail, web design and PPTX; Firecrawl; scheduled jobs; Activities 2 and 3 (single agent).
3. **Hermes Teams — Bots, Group Chats, Kanban & Crons.** Introduction to multiple agents: hub-and-spoke, task decomposition and subagents; bots and group chats (Activity 4); video tools and the Kanban board (Activity 5); content crons (Activity 6); grounding and RAG; evaluation; five principles of AI security.
4. **Bonus (optional) — Paperclip: Kopi Kaki as a One-Person Company.** Install Paperclip; set up the company and the CEO; the CEO hires a Chief of Staff, who hires the marketing team; the org chart; the team makes the video; compare with Hermes Kanban.
