LAB 4 — Activity 4 — Your Social Media Team: Five Bots in One Group Chat
Build a Human-AI Workforce with Autonomous AI Agents (TGS-2024043854)
========================================================================

[1] PROMPT — One prompt: hire the social team and its group chat (12 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop — Mia, folder Desktop/kopi-kaki)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hire my social media team as five bots and put them in one group chat. Workspace: Desktop/kopi-kaki.
1. Create these five bots (Hermes profiles) by cloning your settings. Give each the name, title and description shown, and use its role file as its SOUL.md:
- Rafi · Market Researcher · Finds launch angles, audience insights and facts for Kopi Kaki posts; cites the source file for every fact. (role file souls/rafi.SOUL.md)
- Lin · LinkedIn Writer · Writes the founder's LinkedIn post: story, one proof point, call to action; professional Singapore English. (role file souls/lin.SOUL.md)
- Farah · Facebook Writer · Writes the neighbourhood Facebook post: pop-up dates, the 1 Dec opening, friendly kopitiam voice. (role file souls/farah.SOUL.md)
- Ivy · Instagram Writer · Writes the Instagram hook, caption and hashtags, plus a one-line image brief. (role file souls/ivy.SOUL.md)
- Pixel · Image Creator · Makes one on-brand 1080x1350 image per post and the review gallery that shows every post as it will look. (role file souls/pixel.SOUL.md)
2. Create a group chat called 'Kopi Social Team' with all five bots.
3. Ask each bot to introduce itself to the founder of Kopi Kaki Co. in two sentences.
4. Show me the team as one table: name, title, role file, in the group (yes/no). Never post anything publicly.

[2] PROMPT — Meet your team (5 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop → Bots tab → Rafi (then Lin))
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hi, I'm the founder of Kopi Kaki Co. In two sentences: what is your job on my marketing team, and which files in my Desktop/kopi-kaki folder will you read first?

[3] PROMPT — Post the brief and watch them hand off (15 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop → Kopi Social Team (group chat))
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Team, we launch the Gula Melaka Cold Brew on 1 Dec 2026. Work in Desktop/kopi-kaki and follow AGENTS.md, brand-guide.md and compliance-rules.md.
@rafi research first: write outputs/social/research.md with 3 launch angles, the top 3 things tasters loved (with review IDs) and 5 approved facts with their source file. Then hand off to @lin @farah @ivy.
@lin write outputs/social/linkedin.md, @farah write outputs/social/facebook.md, @ivy write outputs/social/instagram.md — each from research.md and your own role file.
Never post anything. If you are unsure about a fact, ask me with @user.

[4] PROMPT — Ask Pixel for the images and the gallery (8 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop → Kopi Social Team)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
@pixel make one 1080x1350 image for each of linkedin.md, facebook.md and instagram.md with tools/make_post_image.py (Python from .office-venv), save them in outputs/social/images/, then build outputs/social/gallery.html with one card per platform and open it for me.

[5] PROMPT — Review like the founder: APPROVE, EDIT or REJECT (10 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop → Kopi Social Team)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
My decisions:
- LinkedIn: APPROVE
- Facebook: EDIT — @farah put the first pop-up date in the first line and cut the post to 80 words
- Instagram: APPROVE
@pixel when Farah is done, refresh the gallery and record every decision with today's date and my reason in outputs/social/approval-log.md.

[6] PROMPT — Reflect: team versus one agent (7 min)  (paste into: Hermes Desktop → Mia (your first session))
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Compare today's five-bot group chat with you working alone on Day 1: time to first draft, how different the three posts sound, number of my edits, and coordination overhead. Give me a 5-row table and one recommendation: when should Kopi Kaki use a group of bots, and when one agent?
