# DESIGN.md — Kopi Kaki visual identity (for HyperFrames)

## Mood
Warm, nostalgic-modern, appetising. Kopitiam heritage meets café craft.

## Colours
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| kopi-brown | #5B3A29 | backgrounds, headings |
| pandan-green | #4E8A3E | accents, CTA buttons |
| cream | #F6EBD9 | light backgrounds, text on brown |
| gula-amber | #C7782A | highlights, price |

## Typography
Rounded sans-serif (e.g. Nunito / Quicksand) for headlines, 72-96 px; body 48 px minimum.

## Layout
9:16 vertical, 1080x1920. Safe margins 80 px. Max 7 words per text card. Logo bottom-right on cream or brown.

## Motion
Gentle: slow push-ins, pour shots, 0.3-0.5 s eases. No flashing.

## What NOT to do
- No health claims, no halal claims, no competitor names.
- No faces of real customers. No text over busy photos without a backing panel.
- Max 2 emojis. No ALL CAPS sentences.
