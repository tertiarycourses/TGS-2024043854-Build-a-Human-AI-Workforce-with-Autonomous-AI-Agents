# One approved training email

Used once in the Topic 2 setup. Read and send are different permissions.

1. Mia drafts the reply and shows you the exact To, Subject and Body.
2. You check it. Only the trainer-approved test address may receive it; never a customer or personal address.
3. You type SEND. Mia sends it once through the Gmail connector and confirms it is in Sent.
4. Nothing else is sent. If anything changes, Mia shows the new version and waits for a new SEND.
